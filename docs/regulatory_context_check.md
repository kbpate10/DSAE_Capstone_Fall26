## What to change in the team documents
1. Replace "16 to 20 percent" with a sourced figure and base, for example: "about 760,000 acre-feet per year
   in 2027-2028, roughly 27% of Arizona's 2.8 million acre-foot Colorado River allotment (reports range
   from about 20% to 31% depending on the measure)."
2. Say the tax pause applies to **new** exemptions.
3. Correct the three author attributions above and verify CarbonX, PNNL, ITIF and CEPR directly.
4. Confirm the docket number on the ACC eDocket system.
5. Treat the governor's surcharge as proposed unless a bill or rule is found.

wrong, and several figures differ between outlets. Anything marked "Not verified" needs a direct look.
